Research

Interdisciplinary research areas

Connecting molecular biology, microbial systems, computation and bioprocess development.

01

Host–microbe interactions

Mechanisms of microbial colonisation, virulence, secretion systems, immune responses and the transition from symbiosis to pathogenesis.

02

Bioinformatics & genomics

Comparative genomics, functional annotation, NGS analysis, phylogenetics, molecular evolution and systems-level interpretation.

03

Structural & computational biology

Protein modelling, molecular docking, interaction-network analysis and computational characterisation of biological targets.

04

Bioprocess engineering

Microbial transformation, natural-product bioprocessing, value addition to agro-resources and process-oriented biotechnology.

05

Fungal & microbial biotechnology

Biosynthetic pathways, PKS/NRPS systems, microbial metabolites and biotechnology applications of fungi and bacteria.

06

Herbal product development

Evidence-informed formulation and development of herbal beverages, functional products and nature-derived innovations.

Approach

From genome-scale analysis to applied biotechnology

The research programme combines wet-laboratory knowledge with reproducible computational workflows. Key interests include antimicrobial resistance, bacterial secretion systems, natural-product biosynthesis and functional interpretation of microbial genomes.

Applied work extends toward product development, process optimisation and digital tools that make biological interpretation more accessible.
