Research

Research interests and experience

Work spanning bioprocess engineering, biotic–abiotic interactions, molecular markers, bioinformatics and herbal-product development.

01

Bioprocess engineering

Development and scale-up of microbial biotransformation processes, including nature-identical vanillin and natural ferulic acid production from agro-resources.

02

Biotic–abiotic interactions

Research on microbial pathogenicity, plant–microbe interactions, stress biology, disease control and environmentally relevant biological interactions.

03

Molecular markers

Morphometric, micromorphometric and DNA-marker approaches for diversity assessment, species discrimination and molecular systematics.

04

Bioinformatics

Comparative genomics, phylogenetics, molecular evolution, structural analysis and computational interpretation of microbial and plant systems.

05

Microbial virulence

Type III and Type IV secretion systems, bacterial adhesion, virulence determinants, host-cell responses and pathogenic mechanisms.

06

Herbal product development

Development of herbal products and functional beverages, with twelve products reported and commercial linkage through PritMit Biotech Pvt. Ltd.
