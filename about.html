About Dr. Pritam Chattopadhyay

An academic profile at the intersection of botany, computation, microbial biotechnology and translational research.

Academic identity

Bridging fundamental botany with computational and translational science

Dr. Pritam Chattopadhyay is an Assistant Professor of Botany at Maharajadhiraj Uday Chand Women’s College, affiliated with the University of Burdwan. His work integrates biological experimentation, computational analysis and bioprocess development.

His academic activities include undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, research mentorship, scientific writing, curriculum development, institutional service and interdisciplinary collaboration.
