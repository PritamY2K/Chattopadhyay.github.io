About Dr. Pritam Chattopadhyay

Assistant Professor of Botany, researcher, educator, entrepreneur and academic administrator working across botany, biotechnology, bioinformatics and bioprocess engineering.

Academic identity

A career connecting fundamental science, computation and translation

Dr. Pritam Chattopadhyay is an Assistant Professor of Botany at M.U.C. Women’s College, Burdwan, affiliated with The University of Burdwan, West Bengal, India.

His professional profile combines undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, research in microbial and plant systems, bioinformatics, process development, innovation, scientific publishing and institutional leadership.
