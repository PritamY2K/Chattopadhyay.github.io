BIRAC-supported innovation

Agro-waste to high-value ingredients

The Biotechnology Ignition Grant project, “Scale-up of Nature Identical Vanillin Production from Biotransformation of Agro-waste(s),” was incubated at Bio-NEST NIPER-Guwahati with a sanctioned budget of ₹50 lakh.

Project 1 · Ingredient recovery

Natural ferulic acid

Development of a low-cost route for recovering ferulic acid from agro-resources, supporting the conversion of agricultural residues into useful biochemical feedstocks.

20 gExperimental ferulic acid yield per kilogram of agro-waste.

Potential application areas include food ingredients, cosmetics and industrial biochemical intermediates. Further work includes process validation and pilot-scale development.

Project 2 · Microbial biotransformation

Nature-identical vanillin

A microbial process for producing vanillin from ferulic-acid-containing agro-resources, combining bioprocess engineering with strain and enzyme research.

9.9 gExperimental vanillin yield per kilogram of agro-waste.

Development includes strain improvement and characterisation of the biotransformation pathway. The technology assessment records TRL 8, with the underlying process retained as proprietary know-how.

Research to translation

Experimental validation, process optimisation, pilot-scale development and technology transfer.

Project 3 · Herbal tea & herbal drinks

Afepsima

Afepsima is a herbal-tea brand developed around formulation research, a broad selection of blends and the option to tailor products to dietary preferences.

The portfolio includes twelve herbal-tea formulations. The brand’s intellectual-property record includes Trademark No. 5064292, dated 28 July 2021, in Class 30, transferred from Dr. Pritam Chattopadhyay to PritMit Biotech Private Limited.

Project 4 · Beverage development

Non-alcoholic herbal beverage concepts

Research and development of colourful, plant-based beverage prototypes intended for consumers seeking non-alcoholic alternatives.

Development priorities include flavour, appearance, formulation consistency and suitability for food-service applications. Product demonstrations have formed part of the innovation programme.

HerbalNon-alcoholicProduct prototypes

The programme is presented as research and product development; no clinical or therapeutic benefit is implied.

Project 5 · Future research direction

Next-generation human probiotics

An emerging research direction focused on Akkermansia and the human gut microbiome, connecting microbial genomics, host–microbe interactions and translational biotechnology.

The research programme includes a proposed metagenomic study of food and cultural practices among Santal communities in Birbhum, in collaboration with the University of Delhi, and computational investigation of secretion-system apparatus components in Akkermansia species.

Research pathway

From microbiome research to future applications

Genomic characterisation → biological validation → safety and functional assessment → formulation feasibility.

This is a future research programme, not an approved or clinically validated probiotic product.
