Profile

Bridging fundamental botany with computational and translational science

Dr. Pritam Chattopadhyay is an Assistant Professor of Botany at Maharajadhiraj Uday Chand Women’s College, affiliated with the University of Burdwan. His work integrates biological experimentation, computational analysis and bioprocess development to address questions in microbial function, host interaction, natural products and applied biotechnology.

His academic activities include undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, research mentorship, scientific writing, curriculum development and interdisciplinary collaborations. He is particularly interested in converting biological knowledge into accessible analytical tools, reproducible research workflows and socially relevant innovations.
