Professional Research Support

Scientific consultation focused on sound methodology, appropriate analytical strategies and meaningful biological interpretation.

I offer a limited number of professional research consultations, individual mentoring sessions and training programmes for researchers working in biological and life-science disciplines.

The primary objective is to help researchers develop scientifically appropriate and reproducible approaches to biological data analysis, bioinformatics workflow development, research methodology and scientific communication.

Consultation is tailored to the specific research question rather than based solely on the application of individual software tools.

How Consultation Works

A simple and transparent process is followed before commencement of professional consultation.

1

Submit an Enquiry

Briefly describe your research question, requirements and expected outcome.

2

Scope Assessment

The scientific scope, feasibility and type of consultation required are evaluated.

3

Agreement

Deliverables, consultation format, professional fee and expected schedule are agreed in advance.

4

Consultation

Research-oriented consultation, mentoring or training is then undertaken according to the agreed scope.

Professional & Ethical Framework

Consultation is intended to support responsible scientific research and researcher development.

Scientific consultation focuses on methodological guidance, critical assessment, interpretation and researcher training. Academic integrity and appropriate attribution of intellectual contributions are expected in all engagements.

  • No fabrication, falsification or manipulation of research data.
  • No preparation of academic assignments or examinations on behalf of students.
  • No thesis or dissertation ghostwriting.
  • No paid or honorary authorship in exchange for consultation.
  • No guarantee of manuscript publication, journal acceptance or predetermined research results.
  • Appropriate confidentiality will be maintained for legitimate unpublished research information shared for consultation.
