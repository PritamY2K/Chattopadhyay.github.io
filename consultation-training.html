Professional support

Guidance built around the scientific question

I offer a limited number of professional research consultations, individual mentoring sessions and training programmes for researchers in biological and life-science disciplines.

The emphasis is on selecting scientifically appropriate approaches, developing transparent and reproducible workflows, troubleshooting analytical problems and interpreting computational outputs in biological context.

Professional framework

Scientific integrity remains central

Consultation supports legitimate research and researcher development; it does not substitute for the researcher’s academic responsibility.

No fabrication or falsification

Data, analyses and interpretations must remain accurate, transparent and scientifically defensible.

No paid or honorary authorship

Authorship must reflect legitimate scholarly contribution and applicable publication standards.

No thesis or assignment ghostwriting

Mentoring and methodological guidance are offered, but assessed academic work is not completed on behalf of students or researchers.
